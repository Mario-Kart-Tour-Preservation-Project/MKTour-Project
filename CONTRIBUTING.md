# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Mario Kart Tour Preservation Project! This document outlines the requirements, rules, and expectations for all contributions to the project.

First, we highly encourage you to [join our Discord!](https://discord.gg/NYFX97ugZz). Where you'll be able to talk with the community members and get assistance with your contribution.


# Mission
The mission of the Mario Kart Tour Preservation Project is to:

1. Respect the copyrights and other intellectual-property rights of Nintendo and other rights holders.
2. Preserve the Mario Kart Tour mobile game in as close to its original form as possible.
3. Preserve the information and history surrounding the Mario Kart Tour mobile game.
4. Where legally possible, provide a means of accessing and experiencing Mario Kart Tour after the official shutdown date.

# Preservation Integrity
Golden Rule: Preserve things as close to their original form as possible.

- Never fabricate archival evidence.
- Never alter preserved evidence in a way that could cause the alteration to be mistaken for the original.
- Clearly identify anything that has been edited, restored, reconstructed, enhanced, or otherwise modified.
- Clearly distinguish original material from reconstruction, interpretation, transcription, or analysis.
- Preserve metadata when possible.

# Piracy Notice
Piracy is a crime! This project does not and will never support or condone piracy or the violation of copyright in any fashion!
This project will never distribute .ipa or .apk files! Please do not ask for them in any channel relating to this project!

This is our official piracy notice, and it's included here as it is important. Please do not upload assets, .IPA or .APK files, or other items that could be problematic. We don't want someone to be able to shut down the project due to a legal dispute. Please don't give them a reason to raise one. It is better to have a small collection of details surrounding the game than to have nothing at all.

**Prohibited Material**
- IPAs and APKs
- Copyrighted material that the project has not determined it has a legal basis to host.
- Links to external sites that host prohibited material

# Intellectual Property and Licensing
On this note, the project as of writing does not have a license. Until a project license is formally adopted, contributors should not assume that contributions are automatically licensed for unrestricted redistribution or reuse. The project will establish a license and contribution terms before relying on contributions as broadly reusable project material. Once a license is selected, the project will establish how existing contributions will be handled and will communicate those terms to contributors.     
As for usage of other materials, contributors are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or other legal basis to submit material to the project. When in doubt, consult the project maintainers before submitting it. Credit others wherever possible.

The project's license applies only to material for which the project or its contributors hold the necessary rights. It does not grant permission to use Nintendo's trademarks, copyrighted game assets, music, artwork, or other third-party material.

One note is on AI generated content or the usage of AI tools in general. Unfortunately, the creation of many AI tools has lead to widespread theft of content. Contributors should not represent AI-generated material as originating from a particular creator, source, interview, historical artifact, or game asset when that attribution is not genuine.

# AI Use

Artificial Intelligence is a tool like everything else. It can be used responsibly, or it can be used irresponsibly. 
First, you are not required to cite your use of AI tools. This is because an AI is not responsible for its output. You are. If you post something (such as an AI summary of your research), you are attesting that you agree with everything inside of that document and that it is truthful to the best of your knowledge. With that said, I'm sure most would appreciate it if you did cite your use of AI, but it is not required.

Second, let's go over what uses of AI is and isn't allowed:

**Allowed:**
- Summarizing Research
- Writing Documentation
- Voiceovers of Wiki pages (Real voice actors preferred every time)
- Generating and improving code. (But please don't vibe code)
- Assisting in the creation of your own custom media

**Not Allowed:**
- Low-effort AI-generated content (“AI slop” or content that is submitted with little meaningful human effort, review, or contribution.)

AI may assist in producing project material, but submissions must contain meaningful human contribution, review, and responsibility. The project may reject submissions that consist primarily of unreviewed or minimally transformed AI-generated material. AI-generated material must also not be presented as authentic Mario Kart Tour material, historical documentation, interviews, screenshots, assets, or other evidence. AI-generated recreations may be used only when clearly identified as recreations and when their use is appropriate for the project.

AI-generated factual claims must be independently reviewed before being incorporated into project documentation.
