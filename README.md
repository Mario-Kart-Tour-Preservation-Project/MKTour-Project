## About

# Mission 

The mission of the Mario Kart Tour Preservation Project is to:
1) Respect the copyrights and other intellectual-property rights of Nintendo and other rights holders.
2) Preserve the Mario Kart Tour mobile game in as close to its original form as possible.
3) Preserve the information and history surrounding the Mario Kart Tour mobile game.
4) Where legally possible, provide a means of accessing and experiencing Mario Kart Tour after the official shutdown date.

# Rationale
Why preserve a mobile game? We believe that there are 2 main reasons:

## Reason 1 | Preservation of gaming history
Mario Kart Tour is a game that is part of the storied history of the Mario Kart series. A series that is so popular and influential that Mario Kart 8 Deluxe became the best selling game on the Nintendo Switch. This series has dating back to the Super Nintendo that people still play today. The titles that are still online even have alive competitive scenes, such as those hosted by [Mario Kart Central](https://mkcentral.com/en-us). Mario Kart Tour is a part of this history. It released during a time where Mario Kart appeared to have been forgotten by Nintendo. Mario Kart 8 Deluxe had released in early 2017, but many were still looking for a new Mario Kart. Tour filled that gap in a way, by offering brand new tracks, a rotating schedule, and Mario Kart on the phone. Additionally, the game was an important piece in the discussion of monetization in mobile games. Where the game like others on the platform would partake in the "gatcha" mechanic, locking certain characters, carts, and 200cc behind randomized "pipes" or a monthly subscription. By preserving this game and it's story, we wish to also preserve it's impact on the mobile market and how it played a part in the "gatcha" mechanic. Lastly, as Mario Kart fans, there are certain things that are exclusive to this game that will become lost media if this game is lost. Many tracks in the game have a "reverse", "trick", "reverse & trick", or "remix" variations that are exclusive to Tour. One track, Piranha Plant Pipeline has not been brought over to other games, and is now lost to time. We hope to preserve this exclusive content so that people can see what this content was. 

## Reason 2 | The investment of players
Mario Kart Tour was a game downloaded to over [200 million devices.](https://web.archive.org/web/20260801030347/https://sensortower.com/blog/mario-kart-tour-200-million-downloads). Which could lead one to believe that quite a few people played this game. Even towards the end, there was a smaller, but dedicated fanbase that followed the game, with even a [competitive scene building around it.](https://web.archive.org/web/20260905182240/https://mariokarttour.net/) These players have spent both time and money into fleshing out this game. Whether that was in ranking up through Mario Kart Central, the built in multiplayer, completing tours, purchasing characters and carts, or even the Gold Pass subscription. However, once the game is gone, all of that time and money will have gone into nothing. We want to make it so that these dedicated players will still be able to engage with one of their favorite games, and to continue to be able to compete and refine their skills.
